# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Register

brand — club site with live sports data (Mode A, inherited).

## Users

- **Current families** (primary): parents and players of the 10U Squirts and 14U Bantam teams — phone-first, often rinkside — who need the next game, the last score and where they stand.
- **Prospective families**: Long Island parents evaluating a Tier III/A travel program.
- **Sponsors & community**: local supporters following the season.

## Product Purpose

Make Rinx Hockey Club's season impossible to miss: game day first (next game, latest result, standings — for both teams), the club story second. Scope: the homepage and the site chrome (header, navigation, footer) that every page shares.

## Positioning

The only Long Island youth travel club whose site runs on live GameSheet data — scores, standings, a points race and auto-written game updates — not a static brochure.

## Brand Personality

Proud, energetic, community-rooted; arena-signage display typography (display-typography-signature); live scores as content (data-led). Confident and family-friendly — never corporate, never esports.

## Anti-references

- Generic 2026 SaaS hero (centered headline + two pill CTAs + pastel blob) — the "modernise" guardrail.
- Esports / neon gaming aesthetics; chrome-gradient sports templates.
- League-portal tables with 11px type and no hierarchy.
- Stock action-sports photography (the club has real photos — use them).

## Brand Commitments

Name Rinx Hockey Club / Rinx HC; circular crest; navy #041E42 + red #C8102E; Barlow Condensed (display, uppercase) + Barlow (body); strap "Tier III/A • Travel Hockey • LIAHL".

## Evidence on Hand

Real photos (goalie action, B&W huddle, 14U trophy photo, facility, coach headshots); live feeds data/schedule-*.json, standings-*.json, games-*.json. No testimonials, club stats or alumni claims — never fabricate; render gaps as [data-placeholder].

## Product Principles

1. **Lead with game day** — the next puck drop and the last score outrank the club story.
2. **Two teams, one club** — 10U and 14U get equal, parallel paths.
3. **Real over decorative** — real scores, real photos, real coaches; nothing invented.
4. **Rinkside legible** — big numerals, 12px minimum labels, thumb-sized targets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA; contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text; every motion neutralised under prefers-reduced-motion; touch targets ≥ 44px.
