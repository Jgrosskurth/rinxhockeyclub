# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Register

brand. A club marketing and season-information site.

## Users

<!-- _provenance: inferred. Basis: captured nav (Roster, Schedule, Stats, Standings, Tournaments, Clinics, Shop Gear) + homepage content. -->

- **Current families (primary):** parents and players on the 10U Squirts and 14U Bantam teams. In season they need the next game, the latest scores, the schedule, the roster and the stats, often on a phone at the rink.
- **Prospective families:** Long Island parents comparing Tier III/A travel programs. They judge the coaches, the facility and whether the club is organized and winning.
- **Extended community:** sponsors, relatives, opposing teams.

## Product Purpose

The front door of Rinx Hockey Club's season. The homepage should answer "when do we play next and how did we do?" for both teams within the first screen. After that it should make the case for the club: its coaches, The Rinx in Hauppauge, and its pride.

## Positioning

A Tier III/A travel program based at The Rinx (660 Terry Road, Hauppauge, NY), which has two full-size indoor NHL rinks on 97 acres of Hidden Pond Park. It plays in the LIAHL with 10U Squirts and 14U Bantam teams.

## Brand Personality

Proud, competitive, community-rooted. It speaks like a **tabloid sports page** ("14U Remains Undefeated", "Clean Sweep", "heart, hustle, and Rinx pride"). It has a **display-typography signature**: all-caps Barlow Condensed is the club's structural language (wordmark, scorelines, team codes "10U"/"14U", "TIER III/A"). It is **place-led**: the rink diagram and The Rinx facility carry the identity.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Rinx Hockey Club. Facility: The Rinx, Hauppauge, NY.
- Colors: navy `#041E42`, Rinx red `#C8102E`, ice `#E8F0F9`, off-white `#F5F6F8`.
- Type: Barlow Condensed (display, uppercase) and Barlow (body).
- Primary mark: the shield crest. Secondary: the round "The Rinx" seal.
- Signature motifs: the rink diagram and the red-white-red stripe.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real scores: 10U beat Long Beach Lightning 10–2 (Sep 27), North Park 13–0 (Sep 20) and Peconic Wildcats 8–2 (Sep 13). 14U beat Peconic 7–1 (Oct 3) and North Park 5–1 (Sep 26), and earned a comeback tie (Sep 21).
- Next game (GameSheet season 16150): 10U vs Delco Phantoms, Fri Oct 9, 5:05 PM, Hatfield Ice Arena (Mid Atlantic Fall Classic).
- 4 coaches with headshots and roles, 3 team photos, 1 facility photo, address and phone.
- Instagram: instagram.com/rinx10usquirts.
- **Absent, never fabricate:** the 14U next game, season W-L records, standings positions, player counts, testimonials, and tryout or registration dates.

## Anti-references

- Generic-2026-SaaS silhouette (oversized sans hero, two-button pair, serial footer).
- Pastel or playful youth-sports templates that dilute the pro-hockey navy and red.
- Stat-callout "trust bars" of invented numbers.
- Editorial or atelier vocabulary ("the journal", "dispatches"). This is a sports page, not a magazine.

## Product Principles

1. **Season first.** The next game and the latest results lead, for both teams.
2. **Real or placeholder.** Every score, date and name comes from the club's own data. Gaps show as gaps.
3. **One club, two teams.** 10U and 14U get equal billing. Neither hides behind a tab.
4. **Rink-side legible.** AA contrast, large tap targets, and fast on a phone in a cold arena.
5. **The rink is the brand.** Rink geometry is a visible structural motif, not a faint texture.
