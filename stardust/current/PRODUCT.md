# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Register

brand. A club marketing and information site (team news, results, staff, facility), not a tool.

## Users

_provenance: inferred. Basis: the nav (Roster, Schedule, Stats, Standings, Tournaments, Clinics, Shop Gear) and the homepage copy._

- **Current families (main audience):** parents and players on the 10U Squirts and 14U Bantam teams. They come back during the season for the next game, schedule, results, roster and stats, often on a phone at the rink.
- **Prospective families:** Long Island parents looking at Tier III/A travel programs. They want the coaches, the facility and proof that the club is serious and well run.
- **Extended community:** sponsors, grandparents, and opposing teams checking scores.

## Product Purpose

Rinx Hockey Club's home on the web. It shows the season (news, results, schedule and stats for 10U and 14U), introduces the coaching staff and The Rinx facility in Hauppauge, and sends families to clinics, tournaments and team gear.

## Positioning

A Tier III/A travel program based at The Rinx (660 Terry Road, Hauppauge, NY), which has two full-size indoor NHL rinks on 97 acres of Hidden Pond Park. It plays in the LIAHL.

## Brand Personality

_provenance: inferred. Basis: captured voice, type and palette._

Proud, competitive, community-rooted. The voice reads like a sports page: "14U Remains Undefeated", "10U Pours on the Goals", "Clean Sweep", "heart, hustle, and Rinx pride". Visually it borrows from pro hockey: navy and red, heavy condensed all-caps type, a rink diagram, a red-white-red stripe. It is energetic but static. Nothing moves except the news carousel.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Rinx Hockey Club. Facility: The Rinx, Hauppauge, NY.
- Colors: navy `#041E42` and Rinx red `#C8102E`, with an ice-blue tint `#E8F0F9`.
- Type: Barlow Condensed (900/700, uppercase) for display and Barlow 400 for body, both from Google Fonts.
- Marks: a shield crest (header) and a round "The Rinx" badge (hero, footer).
- Signature motifs: the rink diagram and the red-white-red stripe.

## Anti-references

_provenance: inferred._ Generic SaaS landing pages; pastel youth-sports templates; anything that dilutes the navy/red pro-hockey register.

## Product Principles

_provenance: inferred._

1. The season comes first: next game, latest result, standings.
2. Pride without hype: real scores, real people, real rink.
3. One club, two teams: 10U and 14U get equal standing.
4. Rink-side usable: it should work on a phone in a cold arena.
