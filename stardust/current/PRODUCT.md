# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Register

brand — club/team marketing site with live sports data (schedules, results, standings) layered in.

## Users

_provenance: inferred — from nav IA (Roster/Schedule/Stats/Standings per 10U/14U), news copy, and contact/clinics pages._

- **Current families** (primary): parents and players of the 10U Squirts and 14U Bantam teams checking the next game, the latest score, standings, and stats — mostly on phones, often at the rink or on game day.
- **Prospective families**: Long Island parents evaluating a Tier III/A travel program (coaching, facility, culture, how to join/clinics).
- **Sponsors & community**: local businesses and relatives following the season.

## Product Purpose

The club's home on the web: tell families who Rinx Hockey Club is (Tier III/A travel hockey at The Rinx, Hauppauge, NY, LIAHL), and keep the season's live information — schedules, results, standings, stats, tournaments, game updates — current and easy to find. Success = families stop asking "when/where is the next game?" in group chats, and prospects see a serious, proud program.

## Positioning

A youth travel program whose site carries **live GameSheet data** (results, standings, points race, auto-written game updates) — not a static club brochure.

## Brand Personality

_provenance: inferred from captured voice + visual surface._
**Proud, energetic, community-rooted.** Sports-desk headlines ("14U Remains Undefeated", "10U Pours on the Goals"), arena navy + goal-light red, condensed all-caps display type, an ice-rink diagram as the signature graphic. Confident but family-friendly — never corporate, never "esports".

## Anti-references

_provenance: inferred._ Generic SaaS hero templates; stock-photo sports imagery; neon/esports gaming aesthetics; cluttered league-portal tables with tiny type.

## Brand Commitments

- Name **Rinx Hockey Club** / "Rinx HC"; crest logo (circular, red/white/navy).
- Colors: navy `#041E42`, red `#C8102E` (plus ice `#E8F0F9`, off-white `#F5F6F8`).
- Type: **Barlow Condensed** (display, uppercase, 700–900) + **Barlow** (body).
- Tagline/strap: "Tier III/A • Travel Hockey • LIAHL"; "Travel Hockey • Long Island, New York".

## Evidence on Hand

- Real photography: goalie action shot, B&W team huddle, 14U trophy team photo, facility panorama, 4 coach headshots, Instagram game-day graphics.
- Live data feeds: `data/schedule-{10u,14u}.json`, `data/standings-*.json`, `data/games-*.json` (refreshed twice daily).
- No testimonials, stats-of-the-club numbers, or alumni claims captured — must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Game-day first: next game, latest result and standing are the most-requested facts.
2. Two teams, one club: 10U and 14U are equal citizens.
3. Real over decorative: real scores, real photos, real coaches.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first audience (rinkside phones, bright/cold environments) — high contrast and large tap targets matter.
